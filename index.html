Before pickup

  • Wash it so damage is visible
  • Remove toll tags and parking passes
  • Leave about a quarter tank of fuel
  • Disable alarms, note any quirks
  • Remove loose exterior accessories

Personal items

  • Up to 100 lbs in the trunk on most loads
  • Below the window line, secured
  • No valuables, firearms or hazmat
  • Items are not covered by cargo insurance
  • Tell us in advance so we plan weight

At delivery

  • Compare against pickup photos
  • Note anything on the bill of lading

Pricing

What drives the number

Transport pricing is not a mystery — it is distance, size, equipment and timing. Here is exactly how we build your rate.

Distance

Per-mile cost drops as the run gets longer. A 300-mile move costs more per mile than a 2,400-mile one — the deadhead and loading time are the same either way.

Size & weight

A dually or a 36-foot fifth wheel eats the deck space and payload of two sedans. Bigger unit, bigger share of the load.

Equipment

Enclosed runs roughly 40–50% above open. Top-load, winch service and drive-away all change the truck we send.

Timing & lane

Expedited pickup and off-season snowbird lanes carry a premium. Flexible dates on a busy lane save you real money.

Road-ready checklist

We inspect it. Then we tow it.

A trailer that has been parked for two seasons is the single most common cause of a delayed towable delivery. Dry-rotted tires and seized bearings do not announce themselves until they fail.

If your unit needs tires, a bearing pack, a light repair or a brake controller, we can arrange the service before pickup and roll it into your invoice — one call instead of three.

FAQ

Questions we get every day

Hotshot hauling uses a heavy-duty pickup and a gooseneck or flatbed trailer instead of a nine-car semi rig. We carry one to four units per load, which means we can leave as soon as your unit is ready, route directly, and pull into residential streets a 75-foot big rig legally cannot enter.
No. That is a deliberate choice. Big rigs are efficient at nine cars and terrible at one. We specialize in the small, urgent and awkward loads that get bumped down the priority list at a volume carrier.
Three to seven days is ideal and usually gets you the best rate. We regularly cover same-week and next-day requests through expedited service — call dispatch and we will tell you honestly whether we have a truck.
Yes. Cargo insurance is carried on every load and applies from the moment your unit is loaded until it is signed off at delivery. Request a certificate of insurance any time before pickup. Personal items left inside are not covered.
Open handles the overwhelming majority of vehicles and is what dealers use to move new inventory. Choose enclosed for classics, exotics, show cars, fresh paint or anything where a rock chip is not an acceptable outcome. Enclosed typically runs 40–50% higher.
Yes, on winch-equipped equipment. The vehicle must roll, brake and steer. Tell us at booking — a non-runner requires different gear and loading time, and finding out at the curb costs everybody a day.
We bring the tow vehicle and the correct hitch, inspect your trailer for road-readiness, hook up and deliver it as a single dedicated unit. Travel trailers, fifth wheels, cargo, utility, horse and dump trailers all move this way. If the trailer needs tires, bearings or lights, we can arrange service before dispatch.
For motorhomes, box trucks and commercial vans, an insured driver operates the unit to its destination instead of loading it on a trailer. It is often faster and less expensive for large self-propelled units.
No deposit is required to book. Payment is due at delivery unless you are on commercial terms. We accept card, ACH, certified funds and cash on delivery. The price we quote is the price on the invoice.
Someone over 18 needs to release and receive the unit and sign the bill of lading, but it does not have to be you. Family, a neighbor, a shop or a dealer contact all work — just give us their name and number in advance.
